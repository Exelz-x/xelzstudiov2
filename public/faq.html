Frequently Asked Questions

What is XelzStudio?

XelzStudio is a free Roblox audio converter. It takes any YouTube link and converts the audio to be perfectly compatible with Roblox's audio playback engine — handling speed, pitch, and tempo automatically.

Why does Roblox need a special conversion?

Roblox plays audio at a different speed than normal media players. If you upload a song directly, it will sound slower or off-tempo in-game. XelzStudio pre-adjusts the audio so it plays at the correct speed and pitch inside Roblox, saving you the trouble of doing it manually in software like Audacity.

Is XelzStudio free?

Yes, completely free. No account required, no payment, no subscription. Just paste the YouTube link and convert.

What is the maximum audio length?

Videos up to 10 minutes (600 seconds) in length are supported. This covers the vast majority of songs and sound effects used in Roblox games.

What format is the output file?

Output is MP3 at 192kbps, 44.1kHz, stereo — the recommended format for Roblox Studio audio uploads.

Are my files stored on XelzStudio servers?

No. All temporary files are automatically deleted from our servers within 10 minutes of being created. We never permanently store your audio files.

Why did my conversion fail?

Common reasons include: the video is age-restricted, private, region-blocked, or longer than 10 minutes. Try a different video or check that the URL is correct and the video is publicly accessible.

How many conversions can I do?

To ensure fair use for everyone, the limit is 10 conversions per hour per user. This is more than enough for typical use. If you need more, simply wait for the next hour window.

Is it legal to convert YouTube audio?

You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights to use any audio you convert. XelzStudio only accesses publicly available content. Always respect copyright law and YouTube's Terms of Service. Use only royalty-free or content you own for Roblox uploads to comply with Roblox's policies.

Does XelzStudio work on mobile?

Yes! XelzStudio is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. The download will go to your device's default download folder.

Can I use XelzStudio for games other than Roblox?

The conversion is tuned specifically for Roblox's audio engine. Results on other platforms may vary and are not guaranteed.

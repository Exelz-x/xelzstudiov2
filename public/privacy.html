Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

XelzStudio ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our audio conversion service at xelzstudio.vercel.app ("Service").

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

XelzStudio is designed with privacy-first principles:

  • We do not require account registration or sign-in.
  • We do not store your name, email address, or personal identifiers.
  • We do not retain any uploaded or converted audio files after delivery. All temporary files are automatically deleted within 10 minutes of creation.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

2. Automatically Collected Technical Data

Like all web servers, our server logs may temporarily record:

  • Your IP address (used for rate limiting and DDoS protection)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited and timestamps
  • HTTP request and response data

These logs are used solely for security, abuse prevention, and service maintenance. They are retained for a maximum of 7 days and then automatically purged.

3. YouTube URLs You Submit

When you submit a YouTube URL, it is used solely to fulfill your conversion request. The URL is not stored, logged permanently, or shared with any third party beyond the YouTube/Google platform (which you are accessing directly when the public video is retrieved).

4. Cookies

XelzStudio does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies. We may use basic session-level data in browser memory for UI state, which is cleared when you close your browser.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Service accesses publicly available YouTube content via YouTube's public data infrastructure. Your use of YouTube content is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We use Vercel for hosting, which may collect standard server logs per Vercel's Privacy Policy.

6. Security

We implement technical measures to protect the Service, including:

  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Automatic deletion of temporary audio files
  • HTTP security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, etc.)

7. Children's Privacy

XelzStudio is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our Service, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at: privacy@xelzstudio.com
